Using the shared master context, design the complete Reports and Analytics module for the Multi-Tenant SaaS Salon & Spa Management ERP Platform.

Reports must respect:
- Tenant isolation
- Branch permissions
- Business unit permissions
- Role permissions

Design the following report categories:

1. Daily Reports
For reception and branch managers:
- Total sales
- Total customers served
- Number of visits
- Number of services performed
- Number of appointments vs walk-ins
- Average service time
- Average invoice value
- Staff performance summary
- Payment method breakdown
- Outstanding visits
- Cancelled services
- Discounts given

2. Weekly Reports
For owners and managers:
- Revenue by day
- Revenue by branch
- Revenue by business unit
- Revenue by service category
- Commission accrued
- Expenses summary
- Top-selling services
- Staff productivity

3. Monthly Reports
For owners:
- Profit overview
- Revenue vs expenses
- Branch comparison
- Business unit comparison
- Commission payout summary
- Inventory consumption summary
- Customer growth
- Repeat customer rate
- Appointment utilization

4. Yearly Reports
For strategic analysis:
- Annual revenue growth
- Branch growth comparison
- Seasonal trends
- Top services
- Customer retention
- Commission trends
- Expense trends

5. Staff Reports
Design:
- Services completed by staff
- Revenue generated by staff
- Commission earned
- Average service duration
- Utilization rate
- Cancellations/no-shows

6. Service Reports
Design:
- Top services by revenue
- Top services by quantity
- Low-performing services
- Service category performance
- Average duration
- Inventory consumption by service

7. Financial Reports
Design:
- Income summary
- Expense summary
- Payment method summary
- Outstanding balances
- Refunds
- Voided payments
- Commission settlement history

8. Customer Reports
Design:
- New customers
- Returning customers
- Customer visit frequency
- Top customers by spend
- Inactive customers
- Birthday list
- SMS engagement if available

9. Inventory Reports
Design:
- Current stock
- Low stock alerts
- Stock movement
- Consumption by service
- Stock adjustments
- Stock transfers between branches or business units

10. Implementation Requirements

- Database fields needed for reporting
