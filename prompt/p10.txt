Using the shared master context, design the complete Staff Commission System for the Multi-Tenant SaaS Salon & Spa Management ERP Platform.

The commission system must support real salon payroll scenarios.

Example:
Service: Hair Cut
Price: 300 ETB
Commission rule: 30%
Staff commission: 90 ETB

Design the following:

1. Commission Rule Types
Support:
- Percentage of service price
- Fixed amount per service
- Tiered commission
- Service-specific commission
- Staff-specific commission
- Role-based commission
- Business-unit-specific commission
- Branch-specific commission
- Commission after deduction of product cost, if optional

2. Commission Trigger Timing
Support configurable triggers:
- Commission created when service is completed
- Commission created when visit is checked out
- Commission created only after payment is received
- Commission created for partial payment if configured

3. Commission Calculation Flow
Explain:
- How commission is calculated per visit_service
- How multiple staff in one visit are handled
- How discounts affect commission
- How cancelled services affect commission
- How refunded payments affect commission
- How manual adjustments are handled

4. Settlement Cycles
Support:
- Daily settlement
- Weekly settlement
- Monthly settlement
- Manual payout
- Pending settlement
- Approved settlement
- Paid settlement
- Disputed settlement

5. Commission Records
Design database fields for:
- commission_rules
- commissions
- commission_settlements
- commission_adjustments

Include:
- company_id
- branch_id
- business_unit_id
- staff_id
- service_id
- visit_id
- visit_service_id
- rule_id
- base_amount
- commission_amount
- status
- settlement_id
- calculated_at
- approved_at
- paid_at

6. Reporting
Provide:
- Staff commission summary
- Branch commission summary
- Settlement history
- Pending commission report
- Paid commission report
- Commission audit report

7. Security
Define:
- Who can view commission
- Who can edit rules
- Who can approve settlements
- Who can mark settlement paid
- Audit trail requirements

8. Edge Cases
Handle:
- Service completed but not paid
- Partial payment
- Refund after commission paid
- Staff transfer between branches
- Rule changed after service completed
- Duplicate commission prevention
- Commission reversal

9. Example Calculations
Provide calculation examples:
- Percentage commission
- Fixed commission
- Tiered commission
- Discounted service
- Multiple staff in same visit
- Refund scenario

10. Implementation Notes
Provide:
- Laravel event listeners
- Domain service suggestion
- Calculation pseudocode
- Idempotency rules
- Database constraints
- Testing scenarios

Output format:
- Commission rule engine design
- Database schema
- Calculation examples
- Workflow
- Security rules
- Edge cases
- Acceptance criteria
