Using the shared master context, design the complete Appointment, Queue, and TV Display system for the Multi-Tenant SaaS Salon & Spa Management ERP Platform.

The system must support:
- Appointment booking
- Walk-in customers
- Queue numbers
- Staff assignment
- Service assignment
- Waiting area display
- TV display screen
- SMS notifications

Design the following:

1. Appointment Booking
Support:
- Customer appointment
- Service selection
- Staff selection
- Business unit selection
- Date and time
- Duration
- Appointment status:
  - scheduled
  - confirmed
  - arrived
  - in_progress
  - completed
  - cancelled
  - no_show

2. Walk-in Customers
Explain:
- How walk-ins enter the queue
- How they are assigned queue numbers
- How they are merged with appointments
- How priority rules work

3. Queue Management
Design:
- Queue numbering format
- Queue reset rules
- Queue by business unit
- Queue by staff
- Queue by service type
- Estimated waiting time
- Called status
- Serving status
- Completed status
- Skipped status

4. TV Display Screen
Design a TV-friendly page showing:

NOW SERVING
A105
Massage Room 2

Also show:
- Next in queue
- Waiting list
- Business unit name
- Branch branding
- Last updated time
- Optional promotional message

Technical requirements:
- Must work with Livewire polling or lightweight refresh
- Must be suitable for cPanel hosting
- Must avoid heavy infrastructure
- Must be readable from a distance
- Must support fullscreen display

5. Staff Assignment Logic
Explain:
- Auto-assign by skill
- Manual assign by receptionist
- Staff availability
- Staff on break
- Staff with appointment conflict
- Multiple staff for different services

6. SMS Notifications
Support:
- Appointment confirmation
- Appointment reminder
- Customer turn approaching
- Service ready
- Visit completed
- Receipt link or summary

7. Calendar View
Design:
- Day view
- Week view
- Staff timeline
- Business unit filter
- Drag-and-drop if feasible
- Conflict detection

8. Operational Rules
Handle:
- Double booking
- Late customer
- No-show
- Appointment cancellation
- Staff unavailable
- Walk-in priority
- Long-running services
- Multi-service visits

9. Database Impact
Define fields for:
- appointments
- appointment_logs
- queue_entries
- queue_display_settings

10. UI Requirements
Provide:
- Reception appointment screen
- Queue board screen
- TV display screen
- Staff schedule screen
- Livewire components
- Polling strategy
- Status badge design

Output format:
- Appointment workflow
- Queue workflow
- TV display specification
- Database implications
- UI design
- Edge cases
- Acceptance criteria
