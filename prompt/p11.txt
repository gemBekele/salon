Using the shared master context, design the complete Inventory System for the Multi-Tenant SaaS Salon & Spa Management ERP Platform.

The inventory system must support services that consume products.

Example:
Service: Hair Coloring
Consumes:
- Hair dye
- Developer
- Gloves

When the service is completed, the system should automatically deduct inventory.

Design the following:

1. Inventory Structure
Support:
- Inventory items
- Categories
- Units of measure
- SKUs
- Branch-level stock
- Business-unit-level stock
- Optional warehouse/location
- Reorder level
- Cost price
- Selling price if retail is supported

2. Service-to-Inventory Mapping
Design how services consume products:
- One service can consume multiple items
- Each item can have quantity and unit
- Consumption can be optional or mandatory
- Consumption can be branch-specific if needed

3. Stock Transactions
Support:
- Purchase in
- Adjustment in
- Adjustment out
- Service consumption
- Transfer in
- Transfer out
- Return to supplier
- Damage/loss
- Opening stock

4. Automatic Deduction Flow
Explain:
- When stock is deducted
- Whether deduction happens on service completion or checkout
- How to handle insufficient stock
- Whether negative stock is allowed
- How to log every movement
- How to reverse consumption if service is cancelled

5. Inventory Permissions
Define:
- Who can view stock
- Who can adjust stock
- Who can transfer stock
- Who can receive stock
- Who can delete stock records

6. Inventory Reports
Provide:
- Current stock report
- Low stock report
- Stock movement report
- Consumption by service report
- Stock valuation report
- Branch stock comparison

7. Multi-Branch Inventory
Explain:
- Branch-specific stock
- Transfer between branches
- Business unit stock usage
- Central purchasing distribution

8. Alerts
Design:
- Low stock alert
- Out of stock alert
- Expiry alert if future support is needed
- Negative stock warning

9. Database Design
Provide schema impact for:
- inventory_items
- inventory_item_service
- stock_transactions
- stock_levels
- inventory_transfers
- inventory_adjustments

10. Edge Cases
Handle:
- Service uses product but stock is zero
- Product unit conversion
- Partial service completion
- Cancelled service after stock deducted
- Deleted service template
- Stock transferred while visit is active

Output format:
- Inventory architecture
- Stock movement workflow
- Database schema
- Business rules
- UI requirements
- Edge cases
- Acceptance criteria
